## Phase 1: Assumption Reversal (COMPLETED)

### North Star Discovery
**The product is:** A simple, voice-first trading journal in Notion where you capture thoughts and emotions in a structured way — the easiest possible thing to use while trading.

### Ideas Generated

**[Voice #1]: The Passive Listener**
*Concept:* Zavi runs in "ambient mode" during trades — always listening, never interrupting. You talk naturally and Zavi captures the raw audio stream. Post-trade, it structures what you said into emotional state, decision points, and self-talk patterns — WITHOUT you having to categorize anything in the moment.
*Novelty:* Flips voice journaling from "data entry tool" to "ambient emotional capture." Zero-friction capture.

**[Voice #2]: The Dual-Purpose Speak**
*Concept:* Speaking to Zavi mid-trade serves two functions simultaneously: (1) self-regulation through verbalization (proven sports psychology technique), and (2) data capture as a byproduct. The system is designed around the FIRST purpose — keeping you grounded — and the data is a bonus.
*Novelty:* Voice as therapeutic intervention that also generates data, not just an input mechanism.

**[Emotion #3]: The Multi-Layer Emotion Stack**
*Concept:* Emotions captured THREE ways, trader chooses depth per entry: (1) Quick dropdown (Zen/Anxious/FOMO — when busy), (2) Raw voice dump (Zavi captures unfiltered words), (3) Indirect inference (confidence score vs. actual outcomes reveals hidden bias). All three feed the same AI engine, which cross-references them. When dropdown says "Zen" but voice sounds panicked and confidence is 9 on a historically losing setup — the system flags the contradiction.
*Novelty:* Layered emotional capture with contradiction detection. A self-deception radar.

**[Emotion #4]: AI-Built Personal Vocabulary**
*Concept:* Over time, AI stops using generic labels and builds YOUR lexicon. "Cameron's Revenge Mode" follows a specific sequence: morning loss → skip lunch → Impulse 7 → trading smaller timeframes. The system names YOUR patterns and detects them before you do.
*Novelty:* Evolving emotional taxonomy unique to each trader, not static categories.

**[Core #5]: The Voice-to-Structure Dictionary ⭐ KEY INSIGHT**
*Concept:* A personal dictionary/mapping connects YOUR natural vocabulary to Notion database fields. "Hammer" → M5 Anchor. "Blue level" → Level Type: Ludwig Blue. "Feeling calm" → Internal State: Zen. "Chased it" → Patience Grade: F. You just talk naturally, the dictionary translates into structured Notion data automatically.
*Novelty:* The journal adapts to how YOU speak. Zero learning curve — the system learns YOUR language.

**[Core #6]: The Expandable Dictionary**
*Concept:* Dictionary isn't fixed — add new mappings anytime. New phrase like "tilted"? System asks once "what does 'tilted' map to?" You say Internal State: Revenge, and it never asks again. Day 1 needs corrections. Day 30 is seamless.
*Novelty:* System gets smarter the more you use it. Self-improving UX.

**[Core #7]: Entry Types Are Automatic, Not Manual**
*Concept:* You never tell Zavi "this is a trade entry" or "this is an observation." You just talk. The dictionary and context figure out the entry type: Trade (mention entry/exit/position), Observation (mention setup forming), Pass (choosing not to take something), Mindset Check (talking about feelings), Pre-Market (before open). Each type populates different fields in Notion — but you never think about that.
*Novelty:* Removes the biggest friction point — deciding WHAT to record. Everything is worth recording. The system sorts it.

**[Core #8]: The "Passes and Pauses" Log**
*Concept:* Non-trade entries (passes, observations, emotional check-ins) live in the same Notion database as trades. Weekly AI analysis: "You passed on 6 setups this week. 4 would have been losers. Your patience is your edge."
*Novelty:* Shows the value of what you DIDN'T do. Most journals only show what you did.

### Key Course Correction
Cameron redirected session away from feature creep (radar charts, adaptive scoring) back to core vision: **simple, voice-first, easiest to use.** All advanced features should serve that simplicity, not compete with it.

## Phase 2: Role Playing (COMPLETED)

### Persona 1: Cameron — 6:45 AM, Pre-Market

**Realistic voice options explored:**
- Option A (Structured): "Zavi, start session. Feeling rested, 6 out of 10 focus. Today I want to only trade Ludwig Red or LIS levels..."
- Option B (Rambling/Honest): "Zavi, just woke up. Yesterday was rough — gave back 80 points..."
- Option C (Minimal): "Zavi, new session. Target: wait for M1 confirm. That's it."

**What Notion should show after pre-market voice:**
- Fresh journal page auto-created with today's date
- Stated intention captured (e.g., "Ludwig Red/LIS only today")
- Emotional baseline logged
- Reminder from yesterday's data
- Personal rules loaded as checklist

**[Pre #9]: The Intent Contract**
*Concept:* You commit to a rule for the day. Zavi reflects it back: "Got it. Today's contract: M5 Hammer, Ludwig Red or LIS only, wait for M1 confirm. I'll flag anything outside this." That intent becomes the benchmark your post-trade entries are scored against — not a generic rubric.

### Persona 2: Cameron — Mid-Trade, Setup Forming

**The tension:** You need to LOG this but can't look away from the chart.

**Realistic voice:** "Zavi... hammer on M5. Blue level. Watching for M1. Impulse... maybe a 5. Waiting."

**What Notion should do:**
- Trade entry created instantly in background (no confirmation noise)
- M5 Anchor: Hammer, Level Type: Ludwig Blue, Impulse: 5, Status: Watching
- Zavi stays SILENT — no readback, no distraction

**[Mid #10]: Silent Confirm Mode**
*Concept:* During execution window, Zavi defaults to silent. No readback, no confirmation beeps. Just captures. If it needs clarification, it queues the question for AFTER the trade closes — never mid-trade. Preference: "Zavi, go quiet until I say 'trade closed.'"

**[Mid #11]: The Watchlist vs. Trade Distinction**
*Concept:* Two entry states: Watching (setup forming) and Active (in trade). "Watching for M1" = Watchlist. "I'm in" = Active. How long you watch before entering is a patience metric.

---

### Persona 3: Cameron — Just Took a Loss

**Reality:** You probably won't open Notion. You want to stare at the chart or find a recovery trade.

**What Zavi should do:**
- Detect trade closed (loss)
- Wait 90 seconds — give you a beat
- Then quietly prompt: "Trade closed. Whenever you're ready, tell me what happened."

**Realistic voice:** "Zavi... I knew it wasn't right. The M5 wasn't clean but I took it because I was bored. Impulse was probably a 7 but I told myself it was a 4. Honest answer: I chased."

**[Loss #12]: The 90-Second Rule**
*Concept:* Zavi never prompts for post-trade reflection immediately after a loss. Configurable pause (default 90 seconds). The reflection you give after 90 seconds is more honest than the one in 10 seconds.

**[Loss #13]: The Self-Deception Flag**
*Concept:* When you say "I told myself..." or "honest answer..." or "I knew but..." — Zavi flags those phrases in Notion with a subtle marker. Over weeks, your self-deception vocabulary becomes visible. You literally see how often you lied to yourself.

### Persona 4: Cameron — Sunday Weekly Review

**Reality:** You don't want to spend an hour in Notion. You want an answer in 5 minutes.

**Voice:** "Zavi, give me my week. How did I do?"

**[Review #14]: The Spoken Weekly Brief**
*Concept:* Sunday review is a conversation, not a report. Zavi speaks: "This week: 12 trades. Best moment — Tuesday, you passed on the BKBrown setup and it would've been a loss. Worst moment — Friday afternoon, Impulse 8, chased twice. The pattern: your discipline drops after 2 PM. One focus for next week: hard stop at 2 PM." You respond, push back, ask questions. It's a debrief, not a dashboard.

**[Review #15]: The One Metric That Matters**
*Concept:* Weekly review surfaces ONE insight — the thing that, if you fixed it, would improve everything else. Derived from correlating data across the week. Keeps improvement focused, not scattered.

### Persona 5: Zavi — What Does the Voice Assistant Need?

**Core problems for Zavi:**
1. Ambiguity — "I'm in" could mean many things
2. Noise — trading floors, talking to yourself vs. to Zavi
3. Corrections — wrong info sometimes, how to handle?
4. Evolution — vocabulary and rules change over time

**[Zavi #16]: The Wake Word + Context Window**
*Concept:* Lightweight trigger "Zavi, [message]." Context carries forward — if you said "watching M5 Hammer" 30 seconds ago, next message is probably same trade. No need to repeat setup.

**[Zavi #17]: Correction Protocol**
*Concept:* Simple override: "Zavi, correction — [new info]." Flags previous entry as superseded, logs corrected version. No deletion. Correction history is itself data — how often you change initial assessment = confidence/clarity metric.

**[Zavi #18]: The Preference Profile**
*Concept:* Zavi maintains evolving personal profile: your level vocabulary, typical Impulse range, trading hours, session length. When something is outside norm: "You've been at this for 3 hours — that's 90 minutes past your usual session." Not a warning. Just an observation.

## Technical Architecture Research (2026-03-27)

### The 3-Layer Stack

```
YOUR VOICE
    ↓
[Layer 1]  ZAVI  — hears you, converts speech to text
    ↓
[Layer 2]  AI PARSER  — reads the text, extracts structured data
    ↓
[Layer 3]  NOTION API  — receives structured data, writes to correct cells
```

### Zavi Product Details (zavivoice.com)

**What Zavi is:** AI Voice-to-Action OS — desktop (Windows, Mac, Linux) and mobile (Android, iOS)
**Two distinct modes:**
1. **Voice Typing** (Right Ctrl hotkey) — dictates text into whatever app is focused, like a keyboard replacement
2. **Voice Commands / Agent Mode** — sends instructions TO Zavi's AI, which then acts on connected apps (Gmail, Slack, Notion, etc.)

**Notion integration capabilities (confirmed from docs):**
- Search pages and workspaces by voice
- Create new pages hands-free
- Query databases by voice
- Add items to databases by voice
- Read specific pages/blocks aloud

**Pricing:**
- Free tier: 1,000 words/day (sufficient for trading journal)
- Pro: $7.99/mo unlimited
- 27+ app integrations included at all tiers

### Architecture Options (Ranked by Preference)

**Option 1 — Zavi Native (Free, Ideal)**
Voice → Zavi Agent Mode → Notion Database

Set up a Zavi Background Agent with a system prompt containing the Voice-to-Structure Dictionary (Idea #5). Zavi's AI handles parsing and writes structured properties to Notion.

Status: NEEDS TESTING — initial test on 2026-03-27 showed Zavi agent mode processing but not completing the Notion write. Agent UI hung and required restart. Voice commands were registering in general history but NOT in agent history, suggesting routing issue between typing mode and agent mode.

**Option 2 — Zavi Dictation + Free Glue Layer (Fallback)**
Voice → Zavi Voice Typing → Notion "Landing Zone" text block → Parser → Notion Database

Use Zavi's reliable dictation mode (Right Ctrl) to transcribe into a dedicated Notion page. A free automation (n8n self-hosted, or Make.com free tier 1,000 ops/mo) reads the text, calls OpenAI API (~$0.001/entry) to parse keywords, writes structured data to Notion database via API.

More resilient — plain dictation always works even if agent mode breaks.

**Option 3 — Rigid Keywords, No AI (Totally Free)**
Voice → Zavi Voice Typing → Exact phrases → Direct Notion API

Skip AI parsing. Train yourself to speak exact trigger phrases: "M5 Hammer", "Ludwig Blue", "Impulse 6". Can be handled by Notion API or Make.com free tier with simple string matching. Tradeoff: lose natural speech freedom.
